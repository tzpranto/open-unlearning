## Key Findings

1. **BLADE is highly robust on KnowUnDo copyright** — all HM values fall in 0.46–0.49 range (excluding broken eps_mul=0.75)
2. **K=0 slightly outperforms K=3** — best K=0 HM=0.4882 (tau=0.9) vs best K=3 HM=0.4811 (tau=0.1)
3. **MMLU is invariant** (~0.44 across all 80 runs) — unlearning does not degrade general knowledge
4. **tau shows most variation** (K=3 Δ=0.012, K=0 Δ=0.017) but much less than TOFU (Δ=0.045)
5. **eta_in irrelevant at K=0** — identical results across all 8 values (same as TOFU finding)
6. **alpha_dual matters slightly more here than TOFU** (K=3 Δ=0.016) — high values (0.5–1.0) degrade ret_ROUGE
7. **eps_mul=0.75 is broken** — ret_ROUGE=0.0 suggests evaluation artifact, not method failure
