\section{Related Work}
\label{sec:related_work}

\paragraph{LLM unlearning methods.}
Machine unlearning was formalized for classification \citep{cao2015towards, bourtoule2021machine} but does not transfer directly to autoregressive LLMs.
For LLMs, gradient ascent (GA) \citep{jang2023knowledge} maximizes the forget loss but diverges and destroys coherence; GradDiff \citep{yao2024large} adds retain cross-entropy but still degrades.
Preference-based methods---NPO \citep{zhang2024negative}, SimNPO \citep{fan2025simplicity}, NGDiff \citep{bu2025unlearning}---collapse more gracefully but saturate without converging to a well-defined target.
RMU \citep{li2024wmdp} steers activations toward random targets for forget data.
What these approaches share is the absence of a \emph{bounded} forget loss with a per-token stopping criterion: GA diverges, NPO damps but never reaches zero, unclamped entropy \citep{yuan2025closer} pushes indefinitely, and LoKU's \citep{cha2025loku} inverted hinge operates on margins rather than distributional entropy.

\paragraph{Benchmarks.}
TOFU \citep{maini2024tofu} tests factual associations with synthetic biographies and a gold retrained model.
MUSE \citep{shi2025muse} tests naturalistic memorization at scale with scalability/sustainability stress tests.
KnowUndo \citep{tian2024knowundo} targets domain-specific unlearning from LoRA-fine-tuned models.
We evaluate on all three for complementary coverage.

\paragraph{Parameter-efficient unlearning.}
LoRA \citep{hu2022lora} has been applied to unlearning via Fisher-informed initialization \citep{cha2025loku} and importance-weighted masking \citep{jia2024wagle}.
In \method, LoRA serves as a \emph{structural constraint}: the low-rank bottleneck prevents catastrophic drift that occurs with full-parameter methods.

\paragraph{Bilevel and constrained optimization for unlearning.}
BLUR \citep{reisizadeh2026blur} places forget in the lower level and retain in the upper, resolving conflicts via gradient projection.
PDU \citep{entesari2026constrained} uses a linear Lagrangian with logit-margin loss and symmetric dual updates.
\citet{cheng2026unlearning} apply ALM with an equality constraint on vision models only.
\method differs: (a)~an \emph{inequality} constraint that never penalizes retain below $\varepsilon$; (b)~\emph{asymmetric} dual updates ($10\times$ slower decay) producing ratchet behavior; (c)~retain in the \emph{inner loop} (repair-first); and (d)~clamped entropy as the forget loss---bounded with a hard per-token deadzone.
