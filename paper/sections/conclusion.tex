\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

We presented \method, a bilevel optimization framework for LLM unlearning that treats retain preservation as a hard constraint via an augmented Lagrangian.
The method introduces two novel components: (1) a clamped entropy forget loss that provides bounded, self-stabilizing forgetting with per-token dropout, and (2) an asymmetric dual update that permanently calibrates the forget--retain tradeoff after the first constraint violation.
All updates are confined to LoRA adapters, limiting damage radius and providing a free reference model.

On MUSE Books, \method achieves HM=0.798, surpassing the gold retrained model.
On TOFU, \method is competitive with or outperforms PDU across 1B and 3B scales.
Loss dynamics exhibit consistent three-phase behavior across all settings, suggesting the ALM discovers a universal tradeoff point.

\paragraph{Limitations.}
Our evaluation is limited to TOFU and MUSE Books; extending to WMDP (safety-critical unlearning) and larger models ($\geq$7B instruction-tuned) remains future work.
The method has more hyperparameters than single-loss baselines (though auto-epsilon reduces the tuning burden).
We have not yet conducted formal ablation studies isolating each component's contribution.

\paragraph{Future work.}
Extending to MUSE News and WMDP benchmarks; formal ablation study; scaling to 7B+ models; investigating whether the implicit differentiation correction (currently disabled) provides benefits at larger $K$.
