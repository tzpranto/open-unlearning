\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

We presented \method, a bilevel optimization framework for LLM unlearning that treats retain preservation as a hard constraint via an augmented Lagrangian.
The method introduces two novel components: (1) a clamped entropy forget loss that provides bounded, self-stabilizing forgetting with per-token dropout, and (2) an asymmetric dual update that calibrates the forget--retain tradeoff from training dynamics rather than manual tuning.
All updates are confined to LoRA adapters, limiting the damage radius of the unlearning update.

On MUSE Books, \method achieves the highest HM (0.798) among all tested methods.
On TOFU, \method is competitive with PDU across 1B and 3B scales while achieving consistently strong forgetting metrics.
Loss dynamics exhibit a consistent three-phase pattern across all settings, suggesting the ALM converges to a stable operating point for the forget--retain tradeoff.

\paragraph{Limitations.}
Our evaluation is limited to TOFU and MUSE Books; extending to WMDP (safety-critical unlearning) and larger models ($\geq$7B instruction-tuned) remains future work.
The method has more hyperparameters than single-loss baselines (though auto-epsilon reduces the tuning burden).
We have not yet conducted formal ablation studies isolating each component's contribution.

\paragraph{Future work.}
Extending to additional benchmarks (MUSE News, WMDP); formal ablation study isolating each component; scaling to 7B+ instruction-tuned models; multi-seed evaluation with confidence intervals.
